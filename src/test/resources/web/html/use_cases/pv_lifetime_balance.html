Monthly details and sources

How the CO2 saving is counted. Every PV kilowatt-hour that reaches a consumer, used at home or exported, is assumed to displace one kilowatt-hour of grid electricity. Production emissions of the modules and the battery are subtracted. Battery losses reduce the delivered energy.

Seasonal Swiss mix. PV produces mostly in summer, when the Swiss consumer mix is cleanest (about 70 to 90 g/kWh in May to August, 140 to 165 g/kWh in winter). Weighting each month's PV output by that month's mix gives a lower saving than the annual average.

Self-consumption model. Monthly resolution: direct use is capped at the load during PV hours, the battery does at most one full cycle per day and only covers the evening and night load. Real day-to-day weather swings lower the battery share somewhat. Monthly PV and load profiles are assumed typical values for the Zurich region (confidence 0.6).
