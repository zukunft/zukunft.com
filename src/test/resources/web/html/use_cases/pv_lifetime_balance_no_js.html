PV production by orientation the annual PV production: the rated power times the specific yield of the best orientation times the yield factor of the real orientation

PV site intensity formula the embodied emission of the PV system divided by the lifetime production at this site; the multiplication by 1000 converts kg to gram

annual consumption mix by PV weighting the consumption mix of a year as the PV installation uses it: the PV-weighted consumption mix, so that the year row of the monthly table shows the mix that the twelve monthly mixes give with the PV delivery as the weight

annual share formula the share of one year of a CO2-eq total over the lifetime, e.g. the embodied emission of the PV system or the net avoided emission; the result carries the annual word, and the years are the lifetime of the PV installation

autarky formula the share of the household consumption covered by the own PV electricity

battery discharge formula what the battery gives back: the charge times the round-trip efficiency

battery effect formula the effect of the battery on the net avoided emission against the same PV installation without battery, which delivers all its production: the lifetime battery losses do not displace the reference mix and the battery production adds its embodied emission; the division by 1000 converts gram to kg

battery embodied emission formula the production emission of all batteries bought over the lifetime

battery losses formula the battery losses: the charge minus what the battery gives back

delivered electricity formula the PV electricity that reaches a consumer, so the production minus the battery losses

emission payback formula the operating years until the gross avoided emission equals the embodied emission, assuming an even avoided emission per year

export formula the PV production that is neither used directly nor stored goes to the grid

grid import formula the consumption not covered by direct use and battery discharge is bought from the grid

gross avoided emission by PV-weighted consumption mix the same with the Swiss consumption mix weighted by the monthly PV delivery: PV produces mostly in summer, when the Swiss consumption mix is cleanest (about 70 to 90 g/kWh in May to August, 140 to 165 g/kWh in winter), so weighting the PV output of each month by the mix of that month gives a lower saving than the annual average

gross avoided emission by consumption mix the emission of the Swiss consumption mix that the delivered PV electricity displaces over the lifetime; one formula per reference mix, because the mix is the operand; the division by 1000 converts gram to kg

gross avoided emission by production mix the same with the European production mix as the replacement mix

gross avoided emission by residual mix the same with the European residual mix as the replacement mix

lifetime total formula the total of an annual number over the lifetime of the installation, e.g. the PV production or the export; the result carries the lifetime word in place of the annual word of the input, and the years are the lifetime of the PV installation

net avoided emission formula the gross avoided emission minus the embodied emission of the PV system and the battery; the reference mix is selected by the context. Every PV kilowatt-hour that reaches a consumer, used at home or exported, is assumed to displace one kilowatt-hour of grid electricity, the production emissions of the modules and the battery are subtracted, and the battery losses reduce the delivered energy

self-consumption share by dispatch the share of the production used on site; the battery charge counts in full, because the losses happen on site too. The self-consumption model has a monthly resolution: the direct use is capped at the load during the PV hours, the battery does at most one full cycle per day and only covers the evening and night load. Real day-to-day weather swings lower the battery share somewhat. The monthly PV and load profiles are assumed typical values for the Zurich region (confidence 0.6)
